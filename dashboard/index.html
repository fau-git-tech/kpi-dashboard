Why this MRR differs from the Finance report

Finance reports recognized revenue, which spreads annual contracts by day and includes one-time and usage charges. This dashboard reports MRR, the recurring run-rate. Both figures are correct, but they measure different things. The bridge below takes the most recent closed month from Finance's number to this dashboard's number. If any line is not explained here, the definitions have drifted apart and the gap needs investigating.
